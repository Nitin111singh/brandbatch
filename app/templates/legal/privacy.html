Template, to be reviewed for compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act before launch.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, hashed password.

How we use it

Only to run the service: rendering your videos, enforcing plan limits, billing and support. We don't sell your data, and we don't use your videos to train models or for any other purpose.

Retention

Job uploads and outputs are deleted about 48 hours after the job is created. Brand kits stay until you delete them. Account data is deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request.
