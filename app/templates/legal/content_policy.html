Content Policy

BrandBatch is for branding content you own or are licensed to use.

Not allowed

  • Reposting others' content: downloading or reusing other creators' videos without permission and adding your branding to them.
  • Gambling and betting promotion: casinos, betting, satta, real-money gaming and similar brands. Brand kits for these are blocked.
  • Illegal or harmful content: content that is sexually explicit, exploits minors, promotes violence or hate, or is fraudulent, including fake endorsements and scams.
  • Impersonation: using another company's or person's brand without authorization.

Enforcement

We act on reports and may remove content, suspend accounts or terminate repeat violators. Report issues through our report form.
